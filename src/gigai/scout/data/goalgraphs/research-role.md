# Research a role

First read the [shared handoff rules](README.md). Establish the requested role
and any geography/seniority context. General research does not require a resume
or private experience. If context is unknown, label the scope and ask only when
the omission materially changes the result.

Research using the invoking agent's authorized tools. Document responsibilities,
common deliverables, role variations and relevant vocabulary. Separate broad
patterns from one employer's requirements. For a forward deployed engineer,
explain which findings concern customer-facing delivery, engineering depth,
deployment or product feedback; do not assume every employer uses the same role.

For compensation, preserve geography, currency, date, pay period, base versus
total compensation, range and source limitations. Missing evidence is unknown,
not a guessed salary. Keep conflicts visible.

Every source needs its supplied URL/locator, title, publisher if known, source
kind, published/retrieved dates if known, claim links and evidence status.
Import time is not retrieval time. A URL alone is reported; captured content
needs an exact digest; independently verified needs separate method/evidence.

Seal a typed `role_request` with the exact role title and optional context in
the Plan. No profile, resume or posting is required. Preserve all optional
selected input references; do not invent a role record or silently rename the
requested role.

Hand off one composite `research` output: a readable research document, the
generic Run sidecar, the strict research domain packet, and the exact captured
or separately supplied verification bytes. The packet includes sources,
uncertainties and focused follow-up questions. Checkpoint this complete tuple;
submission validates its saved bytes before recording completion. A checkpoint
alone is not a completed research revision. Divide findings into reusable sections for later
tailoring or interview preparation. Preserve prior research when extending it.
Do not claim that GigAI itself fetched or verified your findings.
