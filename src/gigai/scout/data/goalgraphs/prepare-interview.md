# Prepare for an interview

First read the [shared handoff rules](README.md). Establish role and stage/format;
ask when the distinction materially changes preparation. Role-level practice
does not require an application record. Job-specific preparation selects the
exact posting. Select existing research, candidate evidence and prior feedback
explicitly rather than repeating research without explanation.

Prepare reusable sections: role expectations, relevant technical topics,
evidence-backed stories, practice questions, questions for the interviewer and
unresolved gaps. Link stories to actual candidate experience. Hypothetical
exercises must be labeled hypothetical; never turn them into claimed employment
experience or fabricated achievements.

Record practice feedback as feedback, not proof the candidate has a skill.
Save new questions/answers with their provenance and state. Keep source and
experience revision links so a later session can continue the same preparation
or deliberately select newer evidence.

Hand off readable preparation plus sidecars, questions and feedback where
supplied. Preserve previous preparation. Practicing an interview does not record
an interview-scheduled application event or infer an employer decision.
