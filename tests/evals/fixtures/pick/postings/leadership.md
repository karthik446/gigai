About the role
Brightwater Systems makes scheduling software for hospitals. We are hiring a Principal Engineer who leads through other people: you set technical direction for a group of 6 teams, grow its senior engineers and stay close enough to the systems to be trusted.

What you will do
- Lead a team of engineers and own its roadmap from design review to operations.
- Mentor senior engineers and grow them to staff-level scope.
- Set technical direction for a platform that many internal teams build on.
- Raise the reliability bar: SLOs, on-call practice and fewer customer-visible incidents.
- Lead cross-team delivery of compliance work with security and legal partners.
- Represent engineering in public: conference talks, community and open-source work.

Requirements:
- 12+ years of software engineering, with a record of leading teams.
- Hands-on background in distributed systems at scale.
- Experience mentoring engineers at several levels.

Nice to have:
- Healthcare or other regulated-industry experience.
- Experience introducing AI tooling to engineering teams.
