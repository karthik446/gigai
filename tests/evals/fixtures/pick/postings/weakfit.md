About the role
Finmere Bank is a mobile-first bank with 2 million customers. The iOS team owns the app our customers open 5 times a week.

What you will do
- Lead the architecture of the iOS app in Swift and SwiftUI.
- Own app performance: launch time, scroll smoothness and crash-free sessions.
- Build the mobile CI/CD pipeline and speed up builds.
- Work with backend teams on the REST and gRPC APIs the iOS and Android clients call.
- Mentor engineers and set technical direction for mobile.

Requirements:
- 8+ years of iOS development; several apps shipped to the App Store.
- Expert Swift; SwiftUI and UIKit.
- Experience in payments or banking; PCI DSS awareness.

Nice to have:
- Kotlin and Android experience.
- A backend background.
