About the role
Tessellate Commerce runs checkout and order management for 4,000 online shops. The Platform team owns the services every product team depends on: the order event stream, the storage layer, the public APIs and the search behind the merchant console.

What you will do
- Design and operate distributed backend services in Go or Java that stay available under heavy load.
- Own the event backbone on Kafka, with exactly-once handling where money is involved.
- Model and tune data on PostgreSQL for write-heavy workloads.
- Design public APIs over REST and gRPC that are safe to retry.
- Define SLOs with the teams that run on the platform and reduce customer-visible incidents.

Requirements:
- 8+ years building backend systems, with several years at staff level.
- Production experience with Elasticsearch or another search engine: indexing pipelines at scale.
- A record of leading projects across teams and mentoring engineers.

Nice to have:
- Payments or ledger experience; PCI DSS.
- Experience building job queues or workflow engines.
- Redis.
