title: Staff Software Engineer, Core Infrastructure
company: Quillstack
location: Remote, United States

About the team
Quillstack runs a document collaboration service used by 60,000 companies. Core Infrastructure owns the compute platform, the data stores and the delivery pipeline that every product team depends on. As a Staff Software Engineer you will lead the next generation of our platform.

What you will do
- Design and operate distributed systems that serve 500,000 requests per second at 99.99% availability.
- Lead our move to multi-region active-active, including failover design and regular recovery drills.
- Own the delivery platform: Helm charts, ArgoCD pipelines and progressive rollouts with automatic rollback.
- Define SLOs with service owners and build the alerting and incident process around them.
- Drive cost efficiency across our AWS footprint.
- Set technical direction across several teams and mentor senior engineers.

Requirements:
- 10+ years of software engineering experience, including 4+ years on infrastructure or platform teams.
- Expert in Go; comfortable reading Python.
- Deep experience with Kubernetes, Terraform and Linux in production.
- Strong PostgreSQL and Kafka experience at scale.
- Observability practice: Prometheus, Grafana and distributed tracing.
- Experience leading incident response and writing postmortems.

Nice to have:
- Rust.
- eBPF or kernel-level debugging.
- Experience building Kubernetes operators.
- SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit experience.
- Open-source infrastructure projects.

This is a remote role in the United States. Base salary range: $230,000 to $290,000 plus equity and benefits.
