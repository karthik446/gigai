title: Staff Engineer, ML Platform
company: Verdant Health
location: Hybrid, Boston, MA

About Verdant Health
Verdant Health builds clinical decision support used in 300 hospitals. The ML Platform team gives our data scientists a safe, fast path from a notebook to a model serving patients' care teams.

What you will do
- Own the model serving platform on Kubernetes: deployments, shadow traffic, rollback and capacity.
- Build the feature platform: batch and streaming pipelines on Spark and Airflow, with online and offline parity.
- Establish model evaluation and validation gates so no model ships without a reviewed report.
- Work with security and compliance on HIPAA controls: de-identification, access logging and audit trails.
- Grow the team: mentor engineers, lead hiring loops and set technical direction with data science leadership.

Requirements:
- 8+ years of software engineering experience, with 3+ years on ML platform or data infrastructure.
- Strong Python and SQL; experience with Go or Java services.
- Production experience with Kubernetes, Spark and Airflow.
- Experience with model serving and feature stores.
- Experience in a regulated environment (HIPAA preferred).
- Clear written communication and a record of mentoring.

Nice to have:
- Experience with LLM applications and retrieval over clinical text.
- Snowflake and dbt.
- Kafka for streaming features.
- PyTorch.
- A Google Cloud or AWS certification.

This role is hybrid: three days a week in our Boston office. Base salary range: $220,000 to $275,000.
