title: Staff AI Engineer, Agent Platform
company: Northbeam AI
location: Remote, United States

About the role
Northbeam AI builds assistants that complete multi-step work for operations teams. The Agent Platform group owns the runtime every product team builds on: tool calling, retrieval, evaluation and the cost controls around them. We are looking for a Staff AI Engineer to set the technical direction of that platform.

What you will do
- Design and build the agent runtime: tool contracts, retries, budgets and long-running workflows that survive failures.
- Own LLM evaluation for the platform: labelled test sets, regression gates in CI/CD and quality dashboards product teams trust.
- Build retrieval systems (embeddings, hybrid search, reranking) over tens of millions of documents with tight latency targets.
- Reduce inference cost through prompt caching, model routing and batching without losing answer quality.
- Protect agents against prompt injection and unsafe tool use.
- Mentor senior engineers and lead design reviews across product teams.

Requirements:
- 8+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 2 years building LLM applications in production.
- Strong Python; working knowledge of TypeScript.
- Experience running services on Kubernetes with solid observability (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus or similar).
- Hands-on experience with LLM evaluation and with retrieval-augmented generation on PostgreSQL or a vector database.
- A record of leading projects across several teams.

Nice to have:
- Fine-tuning experience with PyTorch.
- Rust or Go for performance-critical services.
- Experience with the Model Context Protocol.
- Open-source work or public talks on LLM systems.
- SOC 2 experience for AI features.

We are remote-first across the United States. The base salary range for this role is $240,000 to $310,000 plus equity.
