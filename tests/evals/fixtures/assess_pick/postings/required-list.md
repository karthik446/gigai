About the role
Quarrystone Energy runs billing and outage software for 140 regional utilities. The Reliability team owns the clusters, the deploy tooling and the incident process for 90 services.

What you will do
- Run the Kubernetes platform that every service deploys to, in two clouds.
- Own the infrastructure code and the checks that gate each change.
- Set SLOs with service owners and lead incident response.
- Keep the event pipeline healthy as volume grows.

Requirements:
- 8+ years in infrastructure, platform or reliability engineering.
- You must have production experience with all of Go, Kubernetes, Terraform and Ansible: each is used daily on this team.
- Experience with a major cloud provider: AWS, GCP or similar.
- Hands-on experience with observability tooling such as Prometheus, Datadog, Honeycomb or Grafana.
- Experience leading incident response and an on-call rotation.

Nice to have:
- Experience with a service mesh (Istio or Linkerd).
- Experience with FinOps or cloud cost reporting.
- A relevant certification (for example AWS or Terraform).
