About the role
Sentrymoor Security sells endpoint detection software to 2,000 companies. The Detection Platform team runs the pipeline that turns 40 billion endpoint events a day into alerts within seconds.

What you will do
- Design and operate the streaming services that ingest and enrich endpoint events.
- Own the storage and search layer analysts query during an investigation.
- Set SLOs for the pipeline and reduce customer-visible incidents.
- Lead designs across teams and mentor senior engineers.

Requirements:
- 10+ years building backend or data-intensive systems.
- Strong Go or Java.
- Experience with distributed data technologies such as Kafka, Elasticsearch, ClickHouse and Cassandra.
- Experience operating services on Kubernetes in a public cloud.
- A record of mentoring engineers and leading technical direction.

Nice to have:
- Experience at a cybersecurity software company.
- eBPF or other endpoint telemetry experience.
