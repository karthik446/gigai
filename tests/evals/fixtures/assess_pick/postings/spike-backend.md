About the role
Corvane Logistics runs the routing and tracking platform behind 3 million parcel deliveries a day. The Platform team owns the control plane, the event backbone and the paved road every product team ships on.

What you will do
- Design and operate distributed backend services in Go on Kubernetes across AWS and GCP.
- Own the Kafka event backbone, including exactly-once processing for billing events.
- Define SLOs, build alerting and lead incident response and postmortems.
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform and release through CI/CD.
- Build service templates and gRPC APIs other teams build on.
- Tune PostgreSQL and Redis for low-latency read paths.
- Mentor engineers and lead technical direction across teams.

What we are looking for
- 10+ years building backend or platform systems.
- Strong Go; PostgreSQL and Kafka in production.
- Kubernetes, Helm and Terraform.
- Observability: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry.
- Experience with Cassandra or another wide-column store.
- Nice to have: Rust; service mesh (Istio or Linkerd); logistics or supply-chain experience.
