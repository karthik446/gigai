About the role
Marrowfield HR builds HR software for 9,000 small businesses. We are forming an Agentic Systems team that puts LLM agents into the product: agents that answer employee questions, prepare payroll corrections and draft policy documents, always with a human checkpoint before anything is written to a customer's account.

What you will do
- Design and build multi-agent systems: planner and specialist agents, tool calling, and durable workflows that survive restarts.
- Own agent evaluation: offline evals, regression suites for every prompt change, and release standards for agent quality.
- Build human-in-the-loop checkpoints and approval gates for irreversible actions.
- Build retrieval (RAG) over customer documents with pgvector or a similar vector store.
- Work across the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs; control cost with prompt caching and model routing.
- Expose internal services to agents safely (Model Context Protocol, permissions, audit).
- Add observability for agent runs: tracing, replay, token and cost accounting.
- Lead technical direction across teams and mentor senior engineers.

What we are looking for
- 10+ years of software engineering, with 2+ years building LLM agents in production.
- Strong Python and TypeScript.
- Experience with agent orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph or Temporal.
- Experience defending agents against prompt injection.
- PostgreSQL; experience operating services on Kubernetes.
- Nice to have: fine-tuning open models; experience in HR or payroll software; SOC 2.
