About the role
Finchley Banking is a mobile-first bank with 2 million customers. The iOS team owns the app our customers open 5 times a week.

What you will do
- Lead the architecture of our iOS app in Swift and SwiftUI, with UIKit where it is still needed.
- Own app performance: launch time, scroll smoothness and crash-free sessions.
- Build the mobile CI/CD pipeline: Xcode Cloud or Fastlane, TestFlight and App Store releases.
- Work with backend teams on the REST and gRPC APIs the app consumes.
- Make the app accessible (VoiceOver, Dynamic Type) and secure (Keychain, biometric authentication).
- Mentor iOS engineers and set technical direction for mobile.

What we are looking for
- 8+ years of iOS development; several apps shipped to the App Store.
- Expert Swift; SwiftUI and UIKit; Combine or Swift concurrency.
- Experience with mobile CI/CD and release management.
- Experience in fintech or payments; PCI DSS awareness.
- Nice to have: Kotlin and Android experience; a backend background.
