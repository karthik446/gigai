- Own the event stream that feeds every store.
- Tune data models and queries for low-latency reads.
- Lead designs across teams.

Requirements:
- 10+ years building backend systems.
- Production experience with Cassandra or MongoDB.
- Production experience with Rust.
- Strong Go, with PostgreSQL and Kafka in production.
- Experience operating services on Kubernetes.

Nice to have:
- gRPC API design.
- Experience with Redis.
