# G45.2 — Bounded Exa Research and Immutable Lead Evidence

**Version:** v0.1.7
**Status:** Proposed target contract — contingent on S43.3 authority-focused Adopt, completed
G43.3, and S45.2 Adopt; not implementation or activation authority
**Depends on:** G43.2, G45, S43.3 authority-focused Adopt, completed G43.3, and S45.2 Adopt
**First consumer:** G46.2 `find-jobs-for-requirements`
**Owns:** Exa policy/action/provenance/accounting details, immutable structural
candidate evidence, and lead-to-input binding mechanics
**Does not own:** generic participant/broker lifecycle (G43.3), job-domain lead
promotion, or Find terminal-result policy (G46.2)

## Outcome

After its three gates complete, G45.2 lets an untrusted G43.3 public-research
participant make a suggestion that GigAI may validate into a Plan-authorized
Exa action. GigAI constructs and preserves the exact private credential-free
request, response, citation, reservation, usage, and lineage evidence for that
action. It can materialize immutable structural job candidates and an explicit
later lead-to-job-description binding.

```text
sealed G43.3 research Run Plan + adopted Exa policy
  -> GigAI validates one untrusted suggestion and reserves its own action
  -> private exact request envelope -> Exa -> validated private response
  -> immutable citation and candidate evidence
  -> G46.2 decides whether a candidate becomes selectable
  -> direct operator selection creates a Tailor input binding
```

G45.2 does not give a model a credential, browser, shell, raw HTTP client, or
provider-selection ability. It does not claim a candidate is relevant, verified,
surfaced, selectable, or current; those are G46.2 decisions. The operator starts
the foreground Run, but does not manually browse job sites.

## Contingent Exa policy

`exa-research-policy.schema.json` has schema ID
`urn:gigai:schema:exa-research-policy:1`. An S45.2 Adopt decision freezes its
first actual policy. Until then, any action, command, or implementation attempt
fails `research_provider_not_approved`; no current draft endpoint, action name,
limit, result-ID behavior, or pricing assumption has authority.

The adopted policy is an immutable content-addressed artifact. It requires
`schema_version`, `policy_id`, `provider_id`, `provider_api_version`,
`capability_id`, `permitted_actions`, `canonical_endpoint_rules`,
`outbound_field_rules`, `result_lineage_rules`, `response_validation_rules`,
`reservation_reconciliation_rules`, `citation_retention_rules`,
`sanitization_rules`, `fixture_replay_identity`, `created_at`, and its exact
content digest. `policy_id` matches `exa_policy_<uuidv4>` and `provider_id` is
exactly `exa`. A sealed Plan carries an exact policy artifact reference and
digest, never only the policy UUID.

S45.2, rather than this target contract, determines the first accepted Exa
endpoint/action shapes, per-action and Run ceilings, result/document limit,
response-byte limit, cost derivation, retry behavior, and retention/terms
boundary. G45.2 validates those adopted facts and cannot widen them. The Exa
credential remains a configured machine-local reference; it is absent from the
policy, Plan, participant process, request artifact, workpad projection,
diagnostic, package, fixture, and committed evidence. There is no fallback to
another provider or unmediated web access.

## Exact request and broker-action evidence

G43.3 associates a captured suggestion with its GigAI-assigned participant,
phase, and public brief, then constructs all request identifiers, action
ordinals, and idempotency. It validates generic action capability, pre-call
reservation, cancellation, and Run-level accounting. G45.2 supplies the Exa
policy validator and records the provider-specific evidence.

Every accepted Exa broker request writes a private
`exa-request-envelope.schema.json` artifact with schema ID
`urn:gigai:schema:exa-request-envelope:1`, at
`runs/<run_id>/research/requests/request_<uuidv4>.json`. It requires
`schema_version`, `request_id`, `run_id`, `run_plan`, `participant_id`,
`broker_request`, `policy`, `provider_api_version`, `endpoint_identity`,
`action_type`, `canonical_payload`, `canonical_payload_sha256`,
`outbound_value_derivations`, and `created_at`. The `canonical_payload` is the
exact credential-free provider payload bytes that GigAI will send; its digest
must equal `canonical_payload_sha256`. GigAI proves byte equality between that
artifact and the outbound request after authentication headers are removed.

Every outbound value must be declared `outbound` in the sealed G46.2
requirements record and carry a locator to its approved source field or a
sealed deterministic transformation of that field. The broker rejects values
derived from private references, local notes, agent memory, diagnostics, raw
prior-provider responses, unsealed text, credentials, suggested raw URLs, or
an undeclared field. A participant-supplied URL, citation, tool result, or
claim is non-authoritative; it cannot form a direct Exa target or citation. An
Exa content/document action may be selected only through a
GigAI-minted opaque result handle from an earlier broker action in the same Run
and allowed by the adopted lineage rule. GigAI, not a participant, resolves
that handle to the provider-specific result identifier inside its private
broker lineage.

Before dispatch, G43.3 reserves the action count, worst-case provider cost,
response bytes, and wall time under the S45.2-adopted rule. After dispatch it
records actual usage and reconciles the reservation. The same idempotency key
returns the prior broker event; a new retry requires a new ordinal/reservation.
An absent or unreconcilable provider report follows the exact adopted no-report
rule; it never turns an already-dispatched action into uncharged capacity.

Each dispatched action writes `exa-research-action.schema.json`, schema ID
`urn:gigai:schema:exa-research-action:1`, under
`runs/<run_id>/research/actions/action_<uuidv4>.json`. It requires
`schema_version`, `action_id`, `run_id`, `gig_id`, `graph_id`, `broker_event`,
`request_envelope`, `policy`, `response`, `citations`, `usage`,
`response_validation`, `created_at`, and `state`. `broker_event`,
`request_envelope`, `policy`, `response`, and each citation are exact artifact
references. `state` is exactly `complete`, `no_results`, `failed`, `blocked`,
or `cancelled`; it describes one action only and does not decide the Find Run's
job-domain terminal result.

Provider bytes are untrusted evidence. After response validation, GigAI may
derive `public-research-evidence-view:1` only under the adopted policy's
sanitization rule. That view retains exact broker-action lineage, sanitized
approved public fields, and GigAI-minted opaque result handles, while excluding
raw response bytes, raw provider result identifiers, raw URLs, raw citations,
credentials, and undeclared fields. A subsequent participant receives the view
only through a new sealed G43.3 public brief; it never receives provider bytes
as action output. It may nominate an exposed opaque handle, which GigAI alone
resolves and validates before it constructs a later request. Provider evidence
cannot alter the Plan, role, broker capability, policy, budget, source scope,
later command, or lead-promotion rules. A malformed response or citation fails
validation before any public evidence view can be written.

## Immutable candidate evidence

`job-lead-record.schema.json` has schema ID
`urn:gigai:schema:job-lead-record:1`, stored under
`runs/<run_id>/job-leads/candidate_<uuidv4>/lead.json`. It is a structural
candidate record, not a selectable lead. It requires `schema_version`,
`candidate_id`, `run_id`, `gig_id`, `graph_id`, `discovery_action`,
`source_url`, `retrieved_at`, `as_of`, `citation_snapshot`,
`normalized_job_description`, `identity_key`, `source_fields`, and
`created_at`. `as_of` is exactly `{ "kind": "published" | "updated" |
"unknown", "value": RFC3339 timestamp | null }`; `value` is null exactly when
kind is `unknown` and is never inferred from `retrieved_at`.

Every candidate field used later for display or promotion has an exact citation
locator. `identity_key` is derived only according to the adopted policy's
public-result rule. Equivalent identity keys may be grouped as deterministic
within-Run duplicate evidence, but no candidate is merged, suppressed, or
promoted across Runs. A G46.2 promotion record, not this structural record,
determines whether a candidate is verified and selectable.

Candidate, citation, and normalized-description snapshots are immutable.
Digest mismatch, unsafe path, missing artifact, or mutation of an existing
snapshot is tampering and fails closed. A later accepted action may produce a
new candidate/snapshot and therefore a new eventual binding; it never changes
an existing candidate snapshot in place.

## Direct lead selection and input binding

`gigai job-lead select LEAD_ID --gig GIG_ID --confirm` resolves `LEAD_ID` only
through a G46.2 promotion record with state `selectable_verified`. Its
`--confirm` must arrive through the direct operator CLI path. A model, broker
request, Plan, proposal, environment default, or earlier confirmation cannot
supply, replay, or infer it.

The command writes a strict `lead-input-binding.schema.json` artifact with
schema ID `urn:gigai:schema:lead-input-binding:1`. It requires
`schema_version`, `binding_id`, `project_id`, `gig_id`, `gig_version`,
`source_run`, `promotion`, `candidate`, `candidate_digest`, `lead_snapshot`,
`normalized_job_description_digest`, `operator_actor`, `confirmed_at`,
`job_description_input`, and `created_at`. `binding_id` matches
`lead_binding_<uuidv4>`. The command validates each exact project/Gig/version/
Run/promotion/candidate relationship before writing.

The `job_description_input` is a private
`urn:gigai:schema:run-input-record:2` record with kind `job_description` and
origin `lead_selection`. It records the immutable candidate reference and
normalized description digest. The enclosing binding points to the input record
and selection authority, avoiding a circular reference. Its idempotency key is
project ID, Gig version, source Run, promotion ID, candidate digest, and
normalized-description digest. Repeating the exact direct confirmation returns
the existing binding; a different immutable candidate produces a new binding.

Selection writes private workpad evidence only. It does not start Tailor,
allocate a Run, invoke a model, grant research/provider permission, mutate an
application state, or make another candidate ambient input.

## Privacy and committed evidence

Exact request envelopes, response bodies, citations, URLs, requirements,
candidate/lead text, local paths, credentials, and raw provider metadata remain
private workpad evidence. They never enter a package, catalog fixture, or Git
closeout artifact.

Committed live-run evidence may contain only policy/API identifiers, sanitized
artifact digests or synthetic IDs, action/byte totals, aggregate usage/cost/
latency, terminal state, mutation-test outcomes, and redacted failure codes.
It must explicitly exclude queries, exact outbound payloads, result URLs,
response bodies, candidate text, requirements, local paths, and credentials.

## Acceptance evidence and stop conditions

G45.2 requires deterministic broker/Exa fixtures and one operator-consented
live Exa execution after all gates. It proves:

- every dispatch has a broker event, exact private request envelope, adopted
  policy reference, reservation, reconciliation, validated response, and
  citation lineage;
- forged, cross-participant, cross-Run, stale, duplicate, malformed,
  undeclared-field, response-derived, over-budget, cancelled, and unaccounted
  requests fail before or at their defined broker lifecycle boundary;
- a public evidence view has exact broker lineage and approved sanitized fields
  but no raw provider response; an unknown, stale, cross-Run, or unexposed
  opaque handle cannot select an Exa content/document action;
- provider prompt-injection content remains evidence and cannot induce a later
  action, privilege, or command;
- a participant-supplied ID, URL, citation, tool result, or claim cannot create
  a request, citation, candidate, or lead without a separately validated
  GigAI-owned broker action and lineage record;
- candidate snapshots are immutable, `as_of` unknown stays unknown, and a
  changed existing snapshot fails rather than creating a binding;
- only a direct operator confirmation of a current G46.2 `selectable_verified`
  promotion creates a binding; self-confirmation, wrong project/Gig/version,
  replay, unsafe path, and digest substitution fail closed; and
- the private evidence and the limited committed closeout obey the exact
  sanitization boundary above.

Stop if G45.2 is activated before S43.3/G43.3/S45.2 complete, a participant
can dispatch Exa outside the broker, a request lacks exact private payload
evidence, query values lack allowed provenance, a result/candidate is promoted
outside G46.2, a direct confirmation can be agent-supplied, or a failure
triggers fallback.
