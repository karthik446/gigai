# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, critique work rather than people,
respect privacy, and make technical disagreement safe and evidence-driven.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private
information, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project
events, and public interactions made on behalf of GigAI.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement should
be proportional, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel rather than a
public issue. If no project-specific channel is available, use the hosting
platform's private reporting and abuse mechanisms.

Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of reporters to the extent
reasonably possible.
